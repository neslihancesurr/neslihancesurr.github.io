We developed a rule-based algorithm that generates morphosyntactic features from finite-state transducer (FST) analyzer outputs and used it to unify Turkish Universal Dependencies (UD) treebanks!

Our related journal paper, *Semi-automated Integration of Morphosyntactic Features Across Turkish UD Treebanks*, is currently under revision. Hopefully, it will be published soon! :sparkles: :smile:
